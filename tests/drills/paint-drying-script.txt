Hello, and welcome to The Paint Drying Podcast. Your host is Gary Bland. Today, we are going to watch paint dry. In my garage, there is a wall. I painted it on Tuesday. The paint is eggshell white, satin finish. Right now, as I speak, the paint is in what experts call the tacky phase. If you touch it, it will feel slightly sticky, and you will regret touching it. In about two hours, it will be touch dry. That means you can touch it, but you probably should not. In about two weeks, it will be fully cured. Cured is the final stage. After that, the wall is just a wall again. Next week, on the podcast, we will discuss a second wall. It is in the hallway. It is not painted yet. Thank you for listening. Please do not touch the wall.
